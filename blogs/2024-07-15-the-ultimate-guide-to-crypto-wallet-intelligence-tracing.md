Wallet Intelligence is the analysis and interpretation of the information (token type, transaction volume, transfer information, etc) associated with a cryptocurrency wallet. With wallet intelligence, you track and understand the behavior, activities, and patterns of transactions made by a specific wallet on the blockchain network. ## Importance of Wallet intelligence Wallet intelligence offers a lot of benefits to crypto users.
