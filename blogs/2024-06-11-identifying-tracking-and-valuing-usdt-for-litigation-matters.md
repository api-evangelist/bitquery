The digital age has ushered in a new era of asset ownership, with cryptocurrency experiencing a surge in popularity. While Bitcoin may be the most well-known, stablecoins like Tether (USDT) increasingly finding their way into investment portfolios. Unlike other cryptocurrencies whose values fluctuate, Tether (USDT) is a stablecoin pegged to the US dollar, offering a supposed one-to-one value.
