On June 5, 2026, Zcash [fell 30 to 50 percent in a single day](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/06/05/zcash-plummets-30-as-developer-reveals-a-major-bug-that-went-undetected-for-four-years). The trigger was the kind of disclosure privacy-coin holders dread. A flaw had been sitting in Zcash's shielded transaction circuit since May 2022, and for those four years it could have let someone mint counterfeit ZEC that nobody would ever see.
