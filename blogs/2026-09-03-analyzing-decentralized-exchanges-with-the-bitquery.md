The 2020 version of this post walked through DEX charts on the old Bitquery explorer, when Uniswap had most of the market and the explorer covered Ethereum alone. Both tools and market have moved on. DEX analysis now lives in [DEXrabbit](https://dexrabbit.bitquery.io/), a dashboard built on the same data as the [DEX API](https://bitquery.io/products/dex) that covers Ethereum, Solana, BNB Chain, Base, Polygon, Tron, Optimism, Arbitrum and Robinhood Chain, while the [Bitquery Explorer](https://explorer.bitquery.io/) covers tokens, addresses and transactions.
