## Background [Syla](https://www.syla.com.au) is Australia’s leading tax software for crypto investments. As a crypto tax software provider, Syla has a need for complete and accurate transaction history imports, which are then utilized for generating tax reports. Facing an increasing demand for accurate on-chain transaction reporting, Syla sought to enhance its [crypto tax calculator](https://bitquery.io/solutions/crypto-tax-software) to provide comprehensive support for additional blockchains.
