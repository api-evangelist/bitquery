### **The Curious Case of a Trader’s Wallet** Crypto markets thrive on volatility, with sharp rises and declines being the norm. At Bitquery, we were analyzing trade data on Solana. We came across a wallet that, at the time of writing, holds [**342 SOL and 254 WSOL**](https://ide.bitquery.io/holdings-of-a-solana-trader-wallet) (tokens that hold real value), also held [**9,290 different memecoin tokens**](https://ide.bitquery.io/number-of-token-accounts-for-solana-trader) at the same time.
